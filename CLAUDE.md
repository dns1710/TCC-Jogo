# Working rules

## Keep output short while working
- Do not narrate individual actions, tool calls or intermediate findings. Work silently.
- Put everything in one final message: what changed, what was verified, what is left, and any question for the user.
- Ask a question mid-task only when blocked on a decision that cannot be resolved from the code or request.
- This applies to OpenSpec workflows too (explore, propose, apply). Their built-in progress output is replaced by the final summary.
